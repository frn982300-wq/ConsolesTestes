FCEUmm Interface Edition integration notice

This package links the FCEUmm-PS2 0.3.3 core as its only emulation core.
The upstream FCEUmm-PS2 source and its license notices are retained in src/core
and LICENSES. The frontend/bridge integration is a separate adaptation.
No game ROMs or BIOS files are included.

The integrated ELF has not yet been built or tested on a PlayStation 2.
